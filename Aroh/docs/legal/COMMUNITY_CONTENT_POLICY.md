## 1. Scope & Applicability

This Community & User Content Policy applies to all user-submitted text, CMS announcements, developer forum discussions, comments, issue reports, and feedback channels across the AROH ecosystem.

---

## 2. Content Standards

All community contributions must remain professional, constructive, and lawful. You shall NOT post or transmit:
1. **Harassment & Hate Speech:** Content that attacks, denigrates, or dehumanizes individuals based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
2. **Defamatory & False Statements:** Unverified allegations, slander, or libel directed at developers, ecosystem contributors, or third parties.
3. **Obscenity & Sexually Explicit Material:** Content containing pornography, sexually explicit depictions, or severe vulgarity.
4. **Commercial Spam & Solicitation:** Unsolicited advertisements, affiliate links, financial scams, crypto-token promotions (outside of standard platform Aros documentation), or repetitive bot messages.
5. **Private Personal Data (Doxxing):** Publishing telephone numbers, residential addresses, personal email addresses, or government identification documents of any individual without explicit consent.

---

## 3. Moderation & Content Removal

1. **Content Review:** AROH administrators reserve the right, but assume no general obligation, to monitor, review, edit, or remove user-generated content that violates this policy or applicable law.
2. **Sanctions:** Violations may result in temporary muting, deletion of posts, suspension of CMS publishing privileges, or permanent account banning.
3. **Appeals:** If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit an appeal to `community@aroh.in` within fourteen (14) days of the action.
