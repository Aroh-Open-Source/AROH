## 1. Ownership of Platform Assets

1. **AROH Core & Shared Packages:** The core hub code (`apps/web`), shared design tokens (`packages/ads`), and core platform contracts (`packages/asdk`) are proprietary assets of AROH, licensed under approved open-source terms where specified in root repository license files.
2. **Trademarks & Brand Identity:** The name "AROH", the official AROH logo symbol, the custom dark/light organic pebble/orb visual language, and the "Aros" token mark are distinctive trademarks of AROH. Nothing in these documents confers any right or license to use AROH trademarks for commercial endorsement without prior written consent.
3. **Independent Product Spoke IP:** Flagship products connected to AROH (including *OmniStream*, *SpeDex*, *Nebula*, *Music Mirror*, and *JavaPath Pro*) remain the separate intellectual property of their respective creators and independent repositories. AROH claims zero ownership over independent product spoke codebases residing within protected boundaries.

## 2. User-Generated Content & Code Submissions

1. **Contributions to Open Source:** Any pull request, issue submission, or documentation contributed to the canonical repository (`https://github.com/Aroh-Open-Source/AROH`) is provided under the repository's declared open-source license.
2. **Representations:** By submitting code or content, you represent and warrant that you own or have obtained all necessary licenses, rights, and permissions to authorize AROH to distribute the contribution without infringing third-party rights.

## 3. Copyright Infringement & Takedown Protocol

AROH respects the intellectual property rights of third parties and complies with notice-and-takedown procedures recognized under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 and Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

If you believe that any material available on the Platform infringes your copyright, please transmit a formal written notice containing:
1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
2. Exact URL or location of the infringing material on the Platform.
3. Your full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
4. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
6. A physical or electronic signature.
