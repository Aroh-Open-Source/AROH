## 1. Statutory Scope & Statutory Exemptions

Under **Section 17 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**, certain provisions of the Act (including notice, consent, and Data Principal rights) do not apply where personal data is processed:
1. In the interest of prevention, detection, investigation, or prosecution of any offence or a contravention of any law in force in India;
2. Pursuant to an order issued by a court of competent jurisdiction or statutory regulatory authority; or
3. For enforcing any legal right or claim.

This policy defines the rigorous standards AROH enforces to verify, review, and respond to legal process received from Indian law enforcement agencies, government bodies, and judicial courts.

---

## 2. Verification Standards for Legal Demands

AROH does not disclose personal data to law enforcement or government authorities without verified, valid legal process conforming to Indian law.

Every request must satisfy the following criteria:
1. **Official Legal Authority:** Must be issued under specific statutory authority (such as Section 91 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 / Section 94 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, or a formal judicial search warrant).
2. **Official Transmission:** Must originate from an official, verified government domain email (e.g. `@gov.in`, `@nic.in`, or official police department domain) or be physically served on official departmental letterhead with the signature and seal of an authorized officer (of rank not below Inspector of Police or equivalent).
3. **Specificity:** Must identify the specific account, email address, transaction identifier, or IP address sought and establish the nexus to the ongoing statutory investigation. Broad, dragnet, or generalized requests seeking bulk user exports will be legally challenged.

---

## 3. Review & Response Protocol

1. **Intake & Triage:** All legal requests are immediately routed to our Legal & Compliance Office at `legal@aroh.in`.
2. **Substantive Review:** Legal counsel audits the facial validity and statutory jurisdiction of the demanding authority.
3. **Data Minimization:** If compelled to comply, AROH releases **only the minimum data strictly required** by the terms of the legal demand (e.g. account registration timestamp and IP logs; passwords and private encryption keys are never disclosed).
4. **Emergency Disclosures:** Where an immediate threat to human life or severe bodily injury is credibly demonstrated, expedited disclosure of necessary subscriber information may be authorized by senior management in consultation with legal counsel.
